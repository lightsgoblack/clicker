Setup, about five minutes

Get Clicker on your Mac and your phone

Clicker is a remote for your TV that runs on your Mac and opens on your phone. One app covers both Apple TV and Roku. It is free, it never leaves your Wi-Fi, and there is no account to make.

Apple TV Roku Mac + iPhone No account

On the Mac

Start here

The Mac does the talking to your TV, so it needs to be awake and on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. 1

    Download it

    Grab Clicker-mac-arm64.zip from the releases page. Unzip it, drag Clicker into your Applications folder, and open it. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

    Download Clicker →

    Older Intel Mac? Use the one-line installer instead, which builds it right on your machine:

    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lightsgoblack/clicker/main/install.sh | bash
  2. 2

    Keep it in the Dock

    Open your Applications folder and drag Clicker onto the Dock. Clicking it starts the remote and opens it in your browser. It runs quietly with no window of its own.

  3. 3

    Connect your TV

    The device list opens by itself the first time. Roku needs nothing: tap Connect. Apple TV asks you to type a PIN off the screen twice, once for the remote and once for apps.

On the iPhone

After the Mac

The phone talks to the Mac, so leave the Mac awake and both on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. 1

    Turn on Party mode

    In Clicker on the Mac, click the QR button in the top right, then switch it On. A code appears.

    Turning it off later locks every phone out again.

  2. 2

    Scan the code

    Point the iPhone camera at it and tap the link. The remote opens in Safari, sized for a phone.

  3. 3

    Add it to the Home Screen

    Tap the Share button, choose Add to Home Screen, then Add. You get the Clicker icon on your Home Screen.

  4. 4

    Open it like an app

    It runs full screen with no browser bar, and it remembers its access, so it keeps working the next time you open it.

One setting on the TV

Most TVs are ready as they are. If buttons do nothing, it is almost always one of these two settings, and each is a one-time change.

Roku

If the remote connects but nothing moves on screen.

Settings › System › Advanced system settings › Control by mobile apps › Network access › Permissive

Apple TV

If no PIN ever appears while pairing.

Settings › AirPlay and HomeKit › Allow Access › Everyone or Anyone on the Same Network

If something is off

No TVs found when scanning
The Mac and the TV have to be on the same Wi-Fi, not a guest network. Wake the TV and scan again.
The phone says Party mode is off
Turn it back on in Clicker on the Mac and scan the code again. The code changes each time, which is what makes turning it off a real lock.
The phone cannot reach it at all
The Mac went to sleep, or Clicker is not running. Open Clicker from the Dock and try again.
Netflix shows no title
Netflix does not tell anything on your network what it is playing. Tap What's on? on the Now Playing card and type it, and the rest of the app catches up.
The app looks like a shrunken desktop page
Safari is in desktop mode for the site. Tap the aA button in the address bar and turn off Request Desktop Website.
